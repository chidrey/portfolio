01PROBLEM

Most CRM products are sized for sales teams with six-figure contracts and dedicated admins. Small agencies and solo consultants end up using spreadsheets — because the overhead of configuring and maintaining enterprise CRM software outweighs the benefit. The result is that relationship data lives in inboxes and heads instead of anywhere useful.

02PROCESS

The product started from a question: what does a solo operator actually need to track? Contacts, companies, deals in some state, and notes. Everything else — lead scoring, pipeline automation, email sequences, forecasting — is for teams with a head of sales to configure and maintain it.

Design work focused on information density: showing enough context per record that you don’t need to click into detail pages constantly. Quick-add flows for contacts and notes so the friction of logging a call or meeting is close to zero.

03SOLUTION

A focused CRM: contacts, companies, deals with pipeline stages, and a notes log per record. No configuration required out of the box. Fast to load, keyboard-navigable, nothing hidden behind settings panels. Built for the person running their own pipeline, not for their sales ops manager.
