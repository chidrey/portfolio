PRODUCT SCREENSHOT
01PROBLEM

Conference badge printing is a logistics problem that gets treated as a print problem. Pre-printed badges mean overruns, waste, last-minute additions handled with hand-written stickers, and check-in queues that back up when someone’s badge can’t be found. The data model — who is attending, in what capacity, with what access level — should drive the credential. Instead, it’s usually the other way around.

02PROCESS

The core design decision was to treat registration data as the source of truth and generate credentials on demand — either at the point of check-in or ahead of time from the latest confirmed list. This eliminates the “batch and pray” pre-print cycle entirely.

Template logic had to be flexible enough to handle the variation that appears in real events: different access tiers, co-located conferences, speakers vs. sponsors vs. staff vs. press. The badge design system needed to express those differences clearly without requiring a separate template for each permutation.

03SOLUTION

A data-driven badge and accreditation system: pull attendee records, apply a parameterized template, generate print-ready output. Check-in captures scan data so accreditation is tracked in real time. Late additions, cancellations, and role changes flow through without touching the design.
