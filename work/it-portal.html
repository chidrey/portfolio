IT Support Portal

An internal support desk built on the tools the company already ran — Azure AD identity, a Google Sheets backend, and automated email — at zero additional running cost.

Fruition Consulting (internal)
ROLE
Designer + developer
YEAR
2024
STATUS
LIVE
PRODUCT SCREENSHOT — SUPPORT DASHBOARD
01PROBLEM

Fruition’s IT support ran through scattered email threads — requests got lost, there was no visibility into what was open or resolved, and no record of recurring issues. The team needed a single place to submit, track, and resolve support requests without buying and administering a full ticketing platform.

02PROCESS

The constraint shaped the architecture: no new infrastructure, no new subscriptions. The stack the company already ran — Microsoft accounts for identity, Google Workspace for data — became the platform. Azure AD handles sign-in so access maps to real staff accounts with zero user management. Google Sheets acts as the database, which means the operations team can read and audit every ticket in a tool they already know.

The interface was designed as a focused dashboard: submit a request, see its status, and for the support side, a queue with state tracking. Apps Script automation closes the loop — confirmation and status-change emails fire without anyone remembering to send them.

03SOLUTION

An internal support portal where staff sign in with their existing Microsoft account, submit requests through a structured form, and get automatic email updates as their ticket moves. The support queue tracks state end to end, and every record lands in a spreadsheet the team can query, filter, and report on directly.

04RESULT

In production at Fruition since 2024. Support requests moved out of inboxes into one tracked queue, with a complete audit trail — built on tools the company already paid for, at zero additional running cost.
